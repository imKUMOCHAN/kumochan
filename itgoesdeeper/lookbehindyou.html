For once I saw a man sharp in gaze and rough in tongue. They looked down on me as I banged on the glass, wanting out. The last bit of dirt thrown on me like I'm some mummy in a casket. "Run along, nothing to see". One spoke out, his head hangs on my wall now... Don't mess with the face that once provoked never leaves the murderous state, for they all now lay lifeless in a bathtub, far from home. I will never see them again.
