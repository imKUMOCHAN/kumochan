AIKUMO // KUMOCHAN

General Info

KUMOCHAN is an ambitious girl, with many talents. She is well known for the creation of the facility THEMAN is currently being held in. Her last whereabouts are however unknown.

Occupation

KUMOCHAN has had multiple occupations over the last few years, however just last year she started as a full-time researcher for the various entities currently being kept, and studied at the Creature Research & Containment Facility Inc.
